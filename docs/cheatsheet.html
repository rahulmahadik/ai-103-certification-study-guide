When to reach for it

Two weeks out
Not yet. Work through the study guide module by module. The sheet is a set of reminders, and it can only remind you of things you have already met.
The week before
One band a day. Cover the right-hand column of each decision table and see whether you can supply it from memory.
The morning of
Scenario cues and key terms only. Those two bands are what turn a half-remembered topic into an answer.

What is on it

  • Exam overviewDuration, pass mark, delivery, renewal and the exam it replaced.
  • Where the marks areThe five skill areas drawn to scale, so you can see that areas 1 and 2 are 55–65% between them.
  • Every sub-skillAll five skill areas with their published bullets, in plain language.
  • Three diagramsHow Foundry is organised, how RAG works, and which optimization lever to pull.
  • Services and syntaxDeployment types, endpoints and SDKs, the four tools, and the prompt patterns.
  • Evaluation and safetyMetric families, the six principles, the four mitigation layers, and the content filter matrix.
  • Scenario cuesThree tables mapping the wording in a question straight to the answer it wants.
  • Key termsThirty-odd definitions, one line each.
  • Not on the sheetExam lineage, prerequisites and when to sit AI-901 first — those are decisions you make once, so they live on the home page.
