# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a study guide, and most people arriving here are trying to
learn something difficult. Assume they are smart and new, not stupid.

## Expected

- Treat questions as genuine, including basic ones. "Read the docs" is not an answer.
- Critique the writing, not the writer.
- Assume good faith when someone disagrees about a technical detail, and settle it
  with a link to the documentation.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Sharing exam questions or dumps, which also breaches Microsoft's exam policies
  and can cost the poster their certifications

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that conflict with this document, and may ban anyone for repeated or serious
breaches.

Report a problem by [opening an issue](https://github.com/rahulmahadik/ai-103-certification-study-guide/issues)
or, if it is sensitive, through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/rahulmahadik/ai-103-certification-study-guide/security/advisories/new).
